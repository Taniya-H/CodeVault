## What are frameworks?
Frameworks are **structured methods or models** used to organize thinking, solve problems, make decisions, or set goals. They give a clear step-by-step approach so work becomes more focused and effective.

---

## 1. SMART Goals
SMART is a framework used to set clear and effective goals.
### SMART stands for:
- **S**pecific – clearly defined
- **M**easurable – can be tracked or measured
- **A**chievable – realistic and possible
- **R**elevant – connected to the main purpose
- **T**ime-bound – has a deadline

### Why it is useful:
- Makes goals clear
- Helps in planning
- Makes progress easier to track
- Increases the chance of success
### Example:
Instead of saying, “I want to study more,”  
a SMART goal would be:  
**“I will study mathematics for 1 hour every day for the next 30 days.”**

---
## 2. Root Cause Analysis
Root cause analysis is a framework used to find the **real cause** of a problem, not just the symptoms.

### Purpose:
To understand **why a problem happened** so that it can be fixed properly and not repeated.

### Steps:
1. Identify the problem
2. Ask why it happened
3. Keep digging until the main cause is found
4. Fix the root cause
5. Check whether the solution worked

### Common method:
**5 Whys Technique**  
Keep asking “Why?” again and again until the root cause is revealed.

### Example:
Problem: A student failed an exam.
- Why? They did not complete the syllabus.
- Why? They wasted too much time.
- Why? They had no study schedule.
- Root cause: Poor time management.

---

## Summary
- **SMART goals** help in setting clear and effective goals.
- **Root cause analysis** helps in finding the real cause of a problem.
- Both frameworks are useful for better planning, decision-making, and problem-solving.
