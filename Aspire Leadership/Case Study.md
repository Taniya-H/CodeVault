
***Between Identity and Individuality Case Study Perspective:***
During an international leadership program, participants spend several weeks working closely in teams, sharing meals, discussing ideas, and learning about each other’s backgrounds. One participant quickly becomes well-known in the group because many people are curious about where they come from. At first, the attention feels positive. People often ask questions about the participant’s language, traditions, childhood, and experiences growing up. As the program continues, the participant begins noticing a pattern. In discussions, people regularly turn to them to explain social issues, cultural expectations, or situations connected to their country and region. Sometimes others introduce them by their nationality before mentioning their name or ideas. During conversations, the participant feels that their personal opinions are often interpreted as representing an entire community rather than simply reflecting one individual's perspective. Although the participant values cultural exchange and understands that curiosity stems from good intentions, they also begin to feel that their identity is slowly becoming larger than their individuality. 
***Aim of Case Study:***
By the end of the program, the participant begins to question whether people truly came to know them as a person, or mainly through assumptions and expectations tied to where they came from.
