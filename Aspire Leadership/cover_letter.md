# Cover Letter Notes

---

## Structure Overview

|#|Section|Goal|
|---|---|---|
|1|Opening Message|Show enthusiasm|
|2|Elaborate on Unique Qualifications|Show value add|
|3|Call to Action|Follow up|

---

## 1. Opening Message — Show Enthusiasm

### What to include:

- **How you found out about the role** — describe the moment/source
- **Why the role or company is attractive** — be specific, not generic
- **Personal touches** — your real interactions with the company or staff (e.g. attended a talk, met someone at an info session)

### Formatting reminders:

- Start with `<Date, Address>`
- Address as `Dear <Hiring Manager>,`

### Sample Write-up:

> `<Company Name>` caught my attention when I first learned about its global macro investment approach and transparent culture. After attending `<Staff Name>`'s talk on the global economy, and meeting `<Staff Name>` at the info session, I became fascinated by `<Company Name>`'s openness and vitality, and eager to contribute to its thought leadership as a Senior Investment Associate.

### Annotations from sample:

- ✅ **Appropriate formatting** — date, address, salutation
- ✅ **How you found out about the role** — first line of the paragraph
- ✅ **Personal touch** — attending a talk, meeting staff at an event
- ✅ **Why you like the company** — openness, vitality, thought leadership

---

## 2. Elaborate on Unique Qualifications — Show Value Add

### What to include:

- Highlight what's **not covered in the resume** — the _"story"_ behind the achievements
    - e.g. the challenges you faced, decisions you made
- **Align with the job description** — explain why your specific skills are a good fit
- **State each skill clearly**, then back it up with how you achieved the result
- Use **active voice** throughout

### Sample Write-up:

> Having worked in various countries and industries, I bring global perspective, and proven analytical skills. As the attached resume details, I believe the following qualities further differentiate me:

- **Strategic thinking:** As part of my recent project in `<country>` to introduce clean water filters, I discovered and implemented ways to incentivize long-term usage of the filters by listening to the villagers and understanding their needs.
    
- **Analytical skills:** At `<current company>`, executives approved the proposals I backed with detailed quantitative analysis. After analyzing the declining engagement in certain markets, I proposed a solution that improved engagement by **20%**.
    

### Annotations from sample:

- ✅ **State the skill clearly** — bold label before each bullet
- ✅ **Share how you achieved the results** — context + actions taken
- ✅ **Use an active voice** — "I discovered", "I proposed", "I backed"

### Formula per bullet point:

```
[Skill Name]: [Situation/Context] → [What you did] → [Result / Impact]
```

---

## 3. Call to Action — Follow Up

### What to include:

- **Reiterate why you like the company** — connect back to their mission
- **Explicitly ask for the interview** — name the role
- **Professional sign-off** — Thank you + Sincerely

### Sample Write-up:

> As a business school student who resonates with `<Company>`'s mission of investing for social good, I would truly appreciate the opportunity to interview for the Senior Investment Associate position. Please feel free to reach out to me if you have any questions.
> 
> Thank you.
> 
> Sincerely, `<Your Name>`

### Annotations from sample:

- ✅ **Reiterate why you like the company** — mission alignment in opening sentence
- ✅ **Call to action** — directly ask for the opportunity to interview
- ✅ **Appropriate formatting** — "Thank you" + "Sincerely" + name

## Quick Reference Checklist

- [ ] Appropriate formatting (date, address, salutation, sign-off)
- [ ] Named the hiring manager
- [ ] Mentioned how you found the role
- [ ] Added a specific personal touch (event, interaction, moment)
- [ ] Stated why you like the company
- [ ] 2–3 skill bullets with stories (not just resume facts)
- [ ] Each bullet: skill → context → result
- [ ] At least one quantified result (%, numbers)
- [ ] Active voice throughout
- [ ] Reiterated company mission in closing

---

## Key Principles

> **Why this company** → Show enthusiasm with specific, real details — not generic praise.

> **Why you** → Tell the story behind the achievement — the challenges, decisions, and context that the resume can't show.

> **Compelling hook** → The opening line should make the reader want to continue. Anchor it to a real, specific moment.


# Cover Letter — Recommended Practices & Examples

---

## Do's and Don'ts

|✅ Do's|❌ Don'ts|
|---|---|
|**Focus on the benefits you can offer** and align with the job description|**Write more than 1 page**|
|**Proofread your drafts** (e.g. use grammar-check tools)|Copy and paste directly from the resume without adding meaningful info|
|Follow company's instructions, if any|**Use the same letter for different applications**|
|**Proactively research** company information and **build connections** with company staff or alumni|Add salary expectations|
|**Use appropriate tone and format**|Overuse the first person narrative (me, myself, I)|
|Write efficiently — be concise|Use too much jargon|

---

## Compare & Contrast

### 1. Opening Message

**❌ Poor Example:**

> To Whom It May Concern:
> 
> I am writing to express my interest in applying for the Marketing Associate position at your company.

**🔧 How to Improve:**

- Use **specific titles or names** — not "To Whom It May Concern"
- Add **personal touches** — share specific motivations for applying to this role
- As a opening message — Use a compelling hook

**✅ Improved Example:**

> Dear Hiring Manager,
> 
> After meeting the Marketing team at the recent recruiting event, I became drawn to the strong culture at your company and have developed a keen interest in applying for the Marketing Associate position.

---

### 3. Call to Action

**❌ Poor Example:**

> I am super confident that I will be a star candidate - this is exactly the job I want! :)
> 
> Please review my resume and call me soon to schedule an interview. I am so eager to meet you!!

**🔧 How to Improve:**

- Avoid **informal verbiage, "texting styles", and emojis** (e.g. `:)`, `!!`)
- Use **exclamation points sparingly**

**✅ Improved Example:**

> I am confident that my strengths and passion for marketing will enable me to effectively contribute to the company's brand.
> 
> I look forward to speaking with you about the opportunity. Thank you for considering my application.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Every letter should be **tailored** — no copy-pasting between applications
- **Research the company** before writing — use specifics to stand out
- Keep tone **professional but warm** — avoid texting language, emojis, and excessive enthusiasm
- **One page max** — be concise, every sentence should add value
- Don't just restate the resume — tell the **story and motivation** behind your experience
- Avoid overusing **"I, me, myself"** — shift focus to what you can offer the company
