**1. What personality traits do you view as important for successful leaders?**  
In my opinion, successful leaders need more than just a growth mindset and the ability to influence others in a positive direction. They should also be open to constructive criticism, coachable, approachable, and genuinely supportive of other people’s growth. A great leader is someone who can contribute to others’ success and still remain supportive even when someone else outshines them.

**2. Which of these traits would you benefit from developing further?**  
I would benefit most from developing a stronger ability to receive criticism as constructive feedback. Being more open to feedback would help me identify my weaknesses more clearly, think more critically, and make better decisions. I also want to become more coachable and approachable, because those traits help me improve consistently and build stronger relationships with the people I work with. In addition, I believe I can grow further in being a supportive leader who helps others improve, especially when they need guidance or when someone else is excelling.

**3. How can this trait or quality be measured? What metrics or feedback will you gather to know if you are exhibiting this quality?**  
These qualities can be measured through regular self-reflection, especially by tracking how my communication with others improves over time and how I respond to feedback. I can also learn by observing effective leaders and studying how they interact with others. Frameworks like root cause analysis can help me assess my behavior more objectively and identify patterns I need to work on.

The three actions I plan to take to strengthen these qualities are:

1. When I receive criticism, I will pause before reacting instead of getting defensive right away. I will try to understand the person’s intention, think about what they are trying to point out, and respond in a constructive and positive way.
2. If a peer outshines me, I will not see it as a threat. Instead, I will treat it as an opportunity to learn from them, build valuable connections, and celebrate their success.
3. I will actively practice building new connections with people on LinkedIn, regardless of their skill level or background. This will help me become more open, collaborative, and supportive as a leader.

Developing this quality is important for both my personal and professional growth because it will help me become a more self-aware, open-minded, adaptable, and effective leader.
