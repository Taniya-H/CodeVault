**Trust and Leadership**

- Trust is a core foundation of effective leadership and teamwork.
- Leaders need trust to build strong relationships, improve cooperation, and achieve long-term success.
- Without trust, communication, collaboration, and organizational growth become weak.

**Historical Importance of Trust**

- Trust has been important since ancient times, not just in modern business.
- Adam Smith argued that markets cannot function properly without fairness, altruism, and trust.
- Antonio Genovesi said public trust holds civil society and the economy together.
- Long-distance trade in early history also depended on trust between people and communities.

**Alibaba Example**

- Alibaba is an example of how trust supports business growth.
- In its early stages, Alibaba built trust among customers and sellers in online commerce.
- By focusing on credibility, customer protection, and transparency, Alibaba gained widespread confidence and became highly successful.
