# Professional Objective – Self-Assessment Rubric
## What is a Self-Assessment Rubric?
A self-assessment rubric is a tool used to evaluate the quality of a professional objective based on specific criteria (key indicators). Performance is measured at different levels:
- **Exemplary** (Excellent)
- **Proficient** (Good)
- **Developing** (Needs Improvement)

---
## Key Indicators
### 1. Concise
**Exemplary**
- Meets the 40-word minimum.
- Does not exceed 75 words or 4 sentences.
- Covers multiple attributes using action verbs and relevant skills.

**Proficient**
- Meets word and sentence limits.
- Mentions skills and goals but lacks depth.

**Developing**
- Does not meet length requirements.
- Fails to cover multiple skills or goals.

---
### 2. Tailored
**Exemplary**
- Integrates skills and experiences unique to the candidate.
- Clearly targeted to a specific industry or company.
- Connects with the goals of the organization.

**Proficient**
- Includes relevant skills and experiences.
- Tailored to a specific industry but not highly personalized.

**Developing**
- Generic objective.
- Does not show clear interest or background in a specific field.

---
### 3. Specific

**Exemplary**
- Includes specific details such as degrees, certifications, or experiences.
- Demonstrates relevant skills and goals.
- Personalized to the candidate.

**Proficient**
- Contains some specific information.
- Skills and goals are relevant but somewhat vague.

**Developing**
- Lacks specific skills, experiences, or goals.
- Does not appear individualized.

---

### 4. Goal-Oriented
**Exemplary**
- Includes clear professional aspirations.
- Connects goals to the mission of the industry or company.
- Shows how the candidate is already working toward those goals.

**Proficient**
- States professional aspirations.
- Links goals to the industry or company.

**Developing**
- Does not include relevant career goals.
- Fails to connect goals with skills, experience, or professional growth.

---

## Quick Summary
A strong professional objective should be:
✅ Concise  
✅ Tailored to the role/industry  
✅ Specific with relevant details  
✅ Goal-oriented and aligned with career aspirations
